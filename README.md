# Netflix Movies & TV Shows Analysis

## Overview
Got my hands on the Netflix titles dataset to see what's actually happening in their library. Worked through cleaning up missing data, fixing date formats, and digging into content trends over the years.

## What I Looked Into
- **Movies vs TV Shows:** Breaking down the ratio of movies to TV shows in the catalog.
- **Content Growth:** How Netflix accelerated its content additions over time.
- **Genre & Ratings Breakdown:** Identifying top genres and common age rating distributions.
- **Top Producing Countries:** Seeing where most of the library's content originates.

## Tech Stack
- **Python**
- **Pandas** (Data manipulation and cleaning)
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** (Data visualization)

## Main Takeaways
- Movies make up the majority of the total catalog compared to TV shows.
- Content additions spiked heavily after 2015, showing Netflix's shift toward aggressive library expansion.
- TV-MA and TV-14 are the most frequent rating categories across the platform.
